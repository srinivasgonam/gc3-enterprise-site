## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a balance between hyper-structured enterprise clarity and cinematic visual storytelling. Tailored for GC³ (Quantocos Global Systems LLP), it serves global executives, IT infrastructure architects, and operational transformation leaders who demand high-tier institutional credibility paired with high-touch creative differentiation.

The visual style merges **Atmospheric Brutalism** and **Executive Luxury**:
- **Atmospheric & Subtractive**: The canvas is anchored in deep cosmic voids, using controlled light leak refractors and spectrum prisms rather than flat lighting or gratuitous fills.
- **Architectural Rigor**: Information density is governed by strict, predictable grid modules derived from complex enterprise suites like ServiceNow, ensuring massive scale and clear navigational hierarchies.
- **Dynamic Editorial Energy**: Large-scale typographic tensions, asymmetric editorial hero layouts, and prismatic spectrum micro-accents break corporate monotony with creative confidence.

## Colors
The palette is deeply rooted in low-light environments, elevated by warm metallic gold core accents and sharp spectral refraction lines.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Containers**:
  - `Void` (`#06060A`): The foundational canvas for global root layouts.
  - `Secondary Dark` (`#0B0B12`): Alternating section containers, page headers, and utility drawers.
  - `Surface` (`#13131C`): Floating cards, modal frames, and interactive cockpit surfaces.
- **Precious Accents (The Gold Scale)**:
  - `Gold Core` (`#C9A44C`): Primary visual hooks, primary call-to-actions, and structural milestones.
  - `Gold Highlight` (`#E8D07C`): Edge bevels, hover illumination, and active state indicators.
  - `Gold Deep` (`#A9852F`): Under-shadows, secondary borders, and muted badge backdrops.
  - `Gold Metallic Gradient`: `linear-gradient(115deg, #F7E9B0 0%, #E2C674 30%, #C9A44C 58%, #A9852F 100%)` for hero headlines, premium badges, and primary keylines.
- **Spectrum Accents (Creative Energy)**:
  - `Spectrum Violet` (`#8B7BFF`), `Spectrum Cyan` (`#38BDF8`), and `Spectrum Magenta` (`#E879F9`).
  - `Spectrum Gradient`: `linear-gradient(90deg, #FF4D6D 0%, #FF8A3D 18%, #FFD166 36%, #7BE495 54%, #38BDF8 72%, #8B7BFF 88%, #E879F9 100%)`. Used with surgical restraint for micro-borders, 1px top-card indicators, and high-energy analytical data streams.
- **Structural Borders**:
  - Gold Accent Border: `rgba(201, 164, 76, 0.18)`.
  - Neutral Structural Border: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.

## Typography
The typography system introduces an interplay of technological precision and editorial authority:

- **Primary Display (Space Grotesk)**: Chosen for its razor-sharp geometric cuts and structural density. Drives primary system headers, dashboard stats, and architectural modules.
- **Editorial Accents (Source Serif 4)**: Applied deliberately to `headline-lg` sub-declarations, pull quotes, and narrative mission headers to establish institutional credibility.
- **Body & Information Delivery (Manrope)**: Delivers geometric neutrality, optimal open counters, and high legibility against deep dark backgrounds.
- **Type Hierarchy Rules**:
  - `label-caps` must strictly be uppercase with `0.12em` tracking, used for technical categories, status tickers, and metadata ribbons.
  - Numbers in data matrices, metrics, and systemic telemetry must use `data-mono` with tabular figures enabled.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout philosophy pairs a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid with asymmetric storytelling breaks.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Architecture**: 12 columns across desktop (`>1024px`) with dynamic gutters (`1.5rem` to `2rem`). Reflows into 6 columns on tablet (`768px - 1023px`) and 4 columns on mobile (`<768px`).
- **Section Pacing**:
  - Macro-transitions between disparate enterprise verticals use `space-3xl` (`6rem`) margins to establish scale.
  - Dense analytical groupings (ServiceNow-grade dashboards, workflow trees) compress down to `space-sm` (`0.5rem`) and `space-md` (`1rem`) internal gaps.
- **Reflow & Docking**:
  - Desktop: Multi-tiered horizontal panels dock into distinct visual ribbons.
  - Mobile: Multi-column data sets convert into vertically scrolling cards with preserved 1px border separation to prevent visual collapse.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is rendered through planar stratification, luminous low-contrast outlines, and ambient color bleeding rather than drop shadows.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Base Level 0)**: Background `#06060A`. Flat, light-absorbing plane.
2. **Structural Tier (Level 1)**: Background `#0B0B12` framed with a subtle border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Used for horizontal bands and side rails.
3. **Card & Panel Layer (Level 2)**: Background `#13131C` framed with `rgba(201, 164, 76, 0.18)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Features an inner hairline highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
4. **Floating Cockpits & Modals (Level 3)**: Background `rgba(19, 19, 28, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a diffused outer glow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 20px -5px rgba(201, 164, 76, 0.12)`.
5. **Spectrum Refraction Edges**: Premium visual feature cards incorporate an ultra-fine 1px gradient keyline (`Spectrum gradient` at 40% opacity) applied exclusively across the top edge or as an active focus trace.

## Shapes
The shape language uses low roundedness (`1` - Soft: `0.25rem` / `4px`) to preserve an architectural, engineered aesthetic. 

- **Micro Components (Chips, Tags, Input Fields)**: Exact `4px` corner radius. Maintains industrial rigidity.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg`)**: `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius. Prevents harshness while retaining alignment with multi-nested panels.
- **Modals & Flyouts (`rounded-xl`)**: `12px` (`0.75rem`) corner radius. Softens elevated focus moments.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Capsule geometry is forbidden for enterprise containers, and strictly reserved for live status beacons and spectrum telemetry tags.
